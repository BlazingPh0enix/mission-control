# Ascent profile (Kerbin, stock aerodynamics)

General community guidance; tune it per rocket.

## Gravity turn
- Launch TWR between 1.2 and 1.8.
- At roughly 50–100 m/s, pitch a few degrees east (heading 90).
- Then follow prograde and let the turn develop; around 45 degrees near 10 km is typical.
- Be nearly horizontal by about 40–50 km.
- Cut the engines when apoapsis reaches about 80 km, coast, and circularize at apoapsis.

## Common problems
- Flipping low in the atmosphere: the turn is too aggressive, or the rocket needs fins at the bottom.
- Going vertical too long wastes delta-v to gravity losses.
- Very high TWR low down wastes fuel fighting drag.

## Staging habits
- Put each decoupler and the next stage's engine in the same KSP stage.
- Keep the parachute in its own, final stage.
