# ReStock

- CKAN id: `ReStock`, version 1.5.1, by ReStock Team
- Kind: Parts (visual replacement). Changes rocket design: No
- Needs: Module Manager
- Needed by: ReStock+, Waterfall - Restock
- Links: [Source](https://github.com/PorktoberRevolution/ReStocked), [Forum or homepage](https://forum.kerbalspaceprogram.com/index.php?/topic/182679-161-restock-revamping-ksps-art/)

ReStock replaces the models and textures of stock parts, through about 330 Module Manager
patches. Its own description: "No effects on gameplay". Parts keep their stock names and stats, so
treat them as stock parts. ReStock+ is the separate mod that adds new parts.

## ReStock: notes

- A part with a `RestockIgnore` key keeps its stock look.
- Waterfall - Restock gives ReStock's engines Waterfall plumes.
