Transfer Window Planner - Fork finds interplanetary launch windows. Unlike the original, it shows
the parking-orbit plane that makes the ejection burn purely prograde, instead of assuming an
equatorial parking orbit with a plane change in the ejection burn. That "can save a lot of Δv"
when the target's orbit isn't in Kerbin's equatorial plane.

## Transfer Window Planner - Fork: notes for the copilot

- The delta-v map's maximum plane-change figures are worst cases. Launching into the plane this
  mod suggests avoids most of that cost.
