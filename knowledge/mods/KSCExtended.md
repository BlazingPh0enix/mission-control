- Needed by: nothing else in this install
- Links: [SpaceDock](https://spacedock.info/mod/2062/KSC%20Extended), [Source](https://github.com/JadeOfMaar/KSC_Extended), [Forum or homepage](https://forum.kerbalspaceprogram.com/index.php?/topic/203623-111-ksc-extended-30/)

KSC Extended adds 20 launch sites in two places, using models from Tundra's Space Center and
Omega's Stockalike Structures. Nine are at the stock KSC, within about 2.5 km of the stock pad. The
other eleven are at a second spaceport, the Tundra Space Center (TSC), at 17.2 degrees north and
88.8 degrees east on Kerbin.

## KSC Extended: notes for the copilot

- The KSC is on the equator. From a TSC site the lowest inclination a rocket can launch into
  directly is about 17 degrees, and Kerbin's rotation gives slightly less help. For equatorial
  targets such as the Mun or keostationary orbit, launch from a KSC site, or budget the plane
  change: about 680 m/s to remove 17 degrees in an 80 km orbit, much less if folded into the
  transfer burn.
- When recommending a pad, check the craft's height and width against its limit. A tall stack
  such as a Ghidorah 9 fits TSC 39A or 39B, which have no limits.
- No site limits part count, and only the helipads limit mass.
