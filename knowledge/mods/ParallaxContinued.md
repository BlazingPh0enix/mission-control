# Parallax Continued

- CKAN id: `ParallaxContinued`, version 1.1.0, by Linx
- Kind: Visuals. Changes rocket design: No
- Needs: Module Manager, Kopernicus Planetary System Modifier, KSPBurst Compiler, Parallax Continued Terrain Textures, Parallax Continued Scatter Textures, Parallax Continued Planet Textures
- Needed by: Parallax Continued Planet Textures, Parallax Continued Scatter Textures, Parallax Continued Terrain Textures
- Links: [Source](https://github.com/Gameslinx/Parallax-Continued), [Forum or homepage](https://forum.kerbalspaceprogram.com/topic/227476-*)

Parallax Continued overhauls the planet terrain shaders and adds grass, forests and other surface
clutter. It needs Kopernicus, KSPBurst and its three texture packs, and recommends Deferred.
Set the game's Terrain Detail to High.

## Parallax Continued: notes

- It doesn't change delta-v or part stats. Its page doesn't say whether surface clutter can
  collide with landers and rovers, so be careful in forests.
