# Kerbal Konstructs

- CKAN id: `KerbalKonstructs`, version v1.12.3.0, by AlphaAsh, ozraven, Ger_space, ryanc55, KSP-RO
- Kind: Launch sites. Changes rocket design: Indirectly
- Needs: Custom Pre Launch Checks
- Needed by: KSC Extended, Omega's Stockalike Structures: No Textures Required, Tundra's Space Center
- Links: [Source](https://github.com/KSP-RO/Kerbal-Konstructs), [Forum or homepage](https://forum.kerbalspaceprogram.com/index.php?/topic/204210-*)

Kerbal Konstructs places static objects on planets: new air bases, launch sites, landing guides
and interactive objects such as fuel stations. It includes an in-game editor for building bases.
In your install, KSC Extended and Tundra's Space Center use it.

## Kerbal Konstructs: notes for the copilot

- Pick a launch site in the VAB or SPH before launching.
- In career mode, new sites start closed and cost funds to open (KSC Extended's sites cost
  from nothing up to 254,100 funds; its note lists each one).
- Sites can set size and mass limits for craft, and can sit away from the equator; KSC
  Extended's note lists its sites.
