# Tundra's Space Center

- CKAN id: `TundraSpaceCenter`, version 2.0.2, by damon
- Kind: Launch sites. Changes rocket design: Indirectly
- Needs: Module Manager, Kerbal Konstructs
- Needed by: KSC Extended
- Links: [SpaceDock](https://spacedock.info/mod/1831/Tundra's%20Space%20Center), [Forum or homepage](https://forum.kerbalspaceprogram.com/index.php?/topic/174368-143-tundras-space-center-v09-april-28th-stockalike-spacex-lc-40-launchpad/&tab=comments#comment-3366232)

Tundra's Space Center provides next-generation launch pad models for Kerbal Konstructs: TLC-5,
TLC-6 and their control building, TLC-14, 17, 19, 34, 39A/B, 40, 41 and 46, horizontal and
vertical engine test pads, and a barge. KSC Extended places these models at the KSC (TSC 39A and
39B, TLC 40 and 41) and at a second spaceport, the Tundra Space Center, at 17 degrees north (the
other TSC sites). The KSC Extended note lists every site.
